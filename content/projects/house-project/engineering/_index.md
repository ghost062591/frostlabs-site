## Engineering (December 2023 - September 2025)

The most extensive phase of the project, spanning nearly 2 years of comprehensive engineering work. This phase involved detailed site design, home specifications, technical planning, and navigating the permit and approval processes.

### Key Activities
- Comprehensive engineering and design work
- Site design and specifications
- Home technical planning and customizations
- Permit applications and approval processes
- Coordination with Clayton Homes and contractors
