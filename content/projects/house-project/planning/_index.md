## Planning & Variance (September 2023)

This phase marks the beginning of our house project journey. In September 2023, we met with the township to secure variance approval for our modular home placement and had our first consultation with Clayton Homes of Paradise PA to explore floor plans and design options.

### Key Activities
- Township variance meeting and approval
- First meeting with Clayton Homes of Paradise PA
- Site assessment and initial planning
- Design selections and preliminary customizations
