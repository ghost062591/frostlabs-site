## Delivery & Installation (October 2025)

The most exciting milestone of the project! In October 2025, after securing our construction loan, the prefab double-wide mobile home was delivered to the site by truck and set on the prepared foundation. This marked the transformation from an empty lot to a standing home.

### Key Activities
- Construction loan finalized (October 2025)
- Prefab double-wide mobile home delivered by truck
- Home set on foundation and leveled
- Initial structural connections and tie-downs
- Weather-proofing and temporary utilities
