## Project Overview

Welcome to my house project gallery! This page documents the journey of our new Clayton Homes prefab double-wide mobile home from initial planning through delivery and installation to the ongoing interior and exterior finishing work.

What started with a township variance meeting in September 2023 has evolved into a comprehensive project involving site preparation, subdivision planning, and the installation of a prefab double-wide mobile home. This gallery showcases the construction progress, design decisions, DIY contributions, and the delivery & installation process.

## Project Phases

### Phase 1: Planning & Approvals (Sept 2023)
- Township variance meeting (September 2023)
- First meeting with Clayton Homes of Paradise PA (September 2023)
- Site assessment and planning
- Design selections and customizations

### Phase 2: Engineering (Dec 2023 - Sept 2025)
- Comprehensive engineering and design work
- Site design and specifications
- Home technical planning
- Permit and approval processes

### Phase 3: Site Preparation (March 2024 - Oct 2025)
- Land clearing and grading
- Site cleaning and preparation
- Subdivision planning and approval
- Installation prep work

### Phase 4: Financing & Delivery (Oct 2025)
- Construction loan acquired (October 2025)
- Prefab double-wide mobile home delivered by truck
- Home set on foundation and leveled
- Initial structural connections and tie-downs
- Weather-proofing and temporary utilities

### Phase 5: Interior Finishing 
- Interior finishing: flooring, painting, fixtures, trim
- Tree planting
- DIY projects and customizations
- Systems testing and final connections

### Phase 6: Site Infrastructure 
- Final grading work
- Stormwater system installation
- Septic system installation
- Utility trenching and connections
- Driveway installation

### Phase 7: Completion 
- Final walkthrough inspection
- Receiving keys
- Move-in ready!
