We've made some great progress on the interior this week! Here's what's been accomplished:

## Flooring

We've started laying down the flooring in the main living area. Going with a luxury vinyl plank that looks like hardwood but is more durable and water-resistant. The color is a nice medium oak that complements the overall design.

## Paint Selection

After many trips to the paint store and holding up dozens of swatches, we've finally settled on our color palette:

- **Living Room & Kitchen**: Warm gray (SW Agreeable Gray)
- **Bedrooms**: Soft blue-gray (SW Rainwashed)
- **Bathrooms**: Crisp white with subway tile accents

## Next Week's Goals

- [ ] Complete living room flooring
- [ ] Start on bedroom flooring
- [ ] Begin painting the main living area
- [ ] Install light fixtures in kitchen

The house is really starting to feel like a home now!
