## Land Clearing (March 2024 - October 2025)

The physical transformation of the site began with extensive land clearing and preparation work. Over 19 months, the property was cleared, graded, and prepared for the modular home installation.

### Key Activities
- Land clearing and tree removal
- Grading and leveling work
- Site cleaning and debris removal
- Subdivision planning and approval
- Installation site preparation
